Presence · brand brief
One brand. Several tracks. A companion, not a tool.
Presence is a job-search companion people run themselves — and, through its tracks, a study companion too. Free forever, local-first, a human clicks every Submit. The brand has to feel like that.
Must express
Calm · honest · present · on your side · owned outright · quietly capable
Must never borrow
Urgency, hype, "10x", countdown energy, spray-and-pray, anything that sells fear to people who are already scared
